A three-phase rollout

NVNM Chain is rolling out in deliberate phases. Each phase unlocks
new participants and new capabilities on the chain. Earlier
participants help shape what the chain becomes.

LIVE May 2026

Closed Beta
Institutional Validation

Audience

Institutional private-markets partners — issuers, oracle validators, and select counterparties. Application-only.

What's Live

The Anchoring Module (EVM precompile + Cosmos messages), hierarchical role-based access control, the typed agent access surface (MCP server at mcp.nvnmchain.io), Inveniam.io as the first integrated data layer, validator set and economic security inherited from MANTRA via Interchain Security, the agentic-accountability whitepaper.

Early institutional participants are recorded on-chain and recognized as foundational contributors in later phases.

NEXT

Open Beta
Developer Ecosystem

Audience

Developers, infrastructure partners, oracle integrators, agent operators, and MCP server builders working on any multi-party agentic workflow.

WHAT UNLOCKS

Developer documentation for the Anchoring Module and the MCP server, additional data-layer integrations beyond Inveniam.io, public RPC, faucets (gas paid in mantraUSD), and a community builder track.

Builders who ship during Open Beta are recognized as ecosystem founders.

FUTURE

Alpha
Beyond Finance

Audience

Operators of multi-party agentic workflows in additional verticals — clinical research, supply chain provenance, regulated AI compute reporting, inter-organizational regulatory reporting — plus retail participants and ecosystem partners.

WHAT UNLOCKS

Multi-party agentic workflows in production across multiple sectors, public dashboards, broader integrations, expanded data-layer participation.

Alpha opens the chain to the wider agentic ecosystem and the communities built around it.

Earlier participants help
shape the chain.
We recognize that.
