Verification without
data exposure

NVNM Chain records cryptographic proofs of asset data while keeping the underlying documents private. This architecture enables auditors, regulators, oracle validators, and institutional counterparties to verify asset information without accessing confidential data rooms.

  • Raw asset data never touches the blockchain

  • Cryptographic hashes enable independent verification

  • Evidence URIs link to governed data environments

  • Security inherited from MANTRA Chain through Interchain Security

Infrastructure for
agentic finance

NVNM Chain enables autonomous agents to analyze, verify, and act on private market asset data. These agents operate over Inveniam IO – managed datasets while using NVNM Chain as the verifiable record of activity, provenance, and compliance.

  • Valuation agents producing near real-time NAV marks

  • Surveillance agents monitoring collateral and covenants

  • Compliance agents automating KYC/AML verification

  • Trading agents executing systematic private asset strategies

Built by leaders

Inveniam is a data infrastructure company for private markets, bringing access, transparency, and trust to asset performance data. The company is building the foundation for scalable AI integration, data marketplaces in private finance, and the systematic trading of private market assets & real-world assets.

MANTRA is an EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain purpose-built for real world assets, with native support for regulatory compliance. As a permissionless chain, MANTRA empowers developers and institutions to participate in the growing RWA tokenization space through advanced modular technology, compliance-ready features, and cross-chain interoperability.
